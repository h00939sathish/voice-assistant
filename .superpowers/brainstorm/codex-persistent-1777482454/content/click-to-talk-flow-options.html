Click-to-talk flow for the AionUi pet

Both options keep Jarvis as the assistant brain. The difference is where microphone capture happens after you click the pet.

A. Pet triggers Jarvis listening
Click pet
or AionUi button
→
Jarvis starts mic
VAD → STT → reply

Trigger Existing Listener

AionUi only sends “wake now”. Jarvis captures the microphone with its current VAD/STT pipeline.

B. Pet records and sends audio
Hold/click pet
record audio
→
Jarvis transcribes
then processes

Send Audio Clip

AionUi owns push-to-talk recording and sends the clip to Jarvis for transcription and response.

Recommended first

  • A is the safer first slice because it reuses Jarvis's existing microphone, VAD, STT, wake handling, and follow-up logic.
  • It avoids fighting over the microphone between AionUi and Jarvis.

Later upgrade

  • B is better if you want true hold-to-talk from inside AionUi or remote WebUI/mobile voice.
  • It needs a proper audio upload/stream endpoint and format normalization.
